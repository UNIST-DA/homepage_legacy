## Motivation
Maritime operations face growing pressure to improve sustainability, with regulations becoming more stringent worldwide.

## Methodology
- Data fusion of AIS trajectories and environmental datasets.
- Regression and neural surrogate modeling for fuel estimation. 

## Contribution (Expected)
- Public-data–based framework for vessel energy consumption.
- CO₂-aware route evaluation for sustainable operations.
