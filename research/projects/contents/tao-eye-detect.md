## Motivation
- Thyroid eye disease is one of the representative complications of thyroid function.
- The ophthalmology department determines the presence or absence of certain symptoms that appear in the early active inflammatory period and imposes a Clinical Activity Score (CAS) so that treatment can begin before irreversible changes occur due to thyroid ophthalmopathy.
- Long-lasting thyroid eye disease can be a cause of great pain associated with Thyroid dysfunction.

## Goal
To develop an AI system for early monitoring and diagnosis of thyroid eye disease during the active inflammatory phase, enabling timely treatment before irreversible changes occur.

## Methodology
Utilizing the power of Deep Learning, we aims to detect all present diseases from eye images.
